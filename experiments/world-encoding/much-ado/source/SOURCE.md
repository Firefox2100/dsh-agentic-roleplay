# Source

- Work: Much Ado About Nothing, William Shakespeare, written about 1598. Public domain.
- Transcription: MIT "The Complete Works of William Shakespeare" (shakespeare.mit.edu), pages much_ado.2.2, 2.3, 3.1 and 3.2, fetched 2026-11-25. Project Gutenberg was down for maintenance that day.
- `text.txt` holds Act 2 scene 2 to Act 3 scene 2 only: the plot against the marriage, and the two staged overhearings. One block per speech (`SPEAKER: lines`), one per stage direction (in square brackets), one per scene heading. Verse line breaks are joined with spaces. The song and the speeches are as transcribed.
- A scene heading and stage directions are the transcription's own, not the play's original text; the place and the time of day are only given by them and by the speeches.
- Check the copyright status in your own jurisdiction before reusing the text elsewhere.
- The transcription gives P203 ("Even she; Leonato's Hero, your Hero, every man's Hero") to Don Pedro. The sense, and Don Pedro's "I will not think it" two lines later, make it Don John's; the encoding follows the sense and lists it in `gaps_found`.
- The text has no stage directions that mark asides. Where the encoding says a line is an aside (P58, P66, P72, P106) that is the encoder's reading from what the listeners do next.
