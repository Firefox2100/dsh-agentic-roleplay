# Source

- Work: The Adventure of the Speckled Band, Arthur Conan Doyle, first published 1892 (The Strand Magazine; collected in The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes).
- Public domain in the United States (published before 1931).
- Transcription: English Wikisource, page "The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes (1892, US)/The Adventure of the Speckled Band", revision 15104245, fetched 2026-11-25.
- `text.txt` is the story text only (page navigation and licence boilerplate removed; the dropped initial of the first word restored). Blank-line paragraph breaks are kept. Two blocks hold two narrative paragraphs each, because the transcription separated them with a single line break: P01 (the preface and "It was early in April") and P195 (the end of Holmes's farewell and "Sherlock Holmes and I had no difficulty"). P194 is a page-heading artefact of the transcription ("GOOD-BYE, AND BE BRAVE").
