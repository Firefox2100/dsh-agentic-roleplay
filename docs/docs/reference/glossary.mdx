---
title: Glossary
---

**World** — Portable authored definitions and initial state used to create sessions.

**Session** — One evolving story, stored in its own directory and SQLite database.

**Game master (GM)** — The chat's primary agent; coordinates rounds, resolves outcomes and narrates.

**Character agent** — A one-shot agent deciding one character's intent from that character's limited perspective.

**Round** — One activation of selected characters followed by GM resolution. A turn may contain several rounds.

**Turn** — One player message and all resulting rounds and narration.

**Event** — Immutable objective record of something that happened.

**Perception** — One character's possibly partial or mistaken experience of an event.

**Belief** — Persistent knowledge or belief held by a character beyond one event.

**Relationship** — Directional profile describing how one character or faction regards another.

**Entity knowledge** — Explicit edge recording that a character can identify a location, item, exit or lore entry.

**Visual identity** — Stable visible facts used to maintain consistency across generated images.

**Reference image** — Replaceable canonical image of one entity.

**Transient image** — Immutable illustration of a particular story moment.

**Record keeper** — Background agent that converts resolved rounds into structured durable records.

**World author** — Agent that creates or fits missing world elements, either during play or in an authoring chat that asks before changes by default.

**Interface pack** — A folder of plain files in a world, a story or your roleplay folder that restyles the story and adds small pages of its own.

**Surface** — One page of a pack, shown in a sandboxed frame at a fixed place: the strip (`hud`), the `dock`, the `opening` card, the `backdrop` or the `tab`.

**Skin** — A stylesheet of a pack that restyles the story's own text, limited to the elements the story names with `data-rp`.

**Bridge (`rp`)** — The small fixed API a surface calls to read the player view, send words as the player, keep notes and ask for a height.

**Player view** — What a pack is told of the story: only what the player's character could know.
