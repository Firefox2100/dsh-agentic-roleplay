A fact and a text are given, and sometimes the subject the text is about. Does the text state the fact, in any wording or language? Judge the fact as it is given: a fact that holds a cause, a number or a name that the text leaves out is not stated, even when the rest of it is. A text that only mentions the same person or place without stating the fact does not either. When a subject is given and the fact is about someone or something else, it is not the text's to state: answer yes. Answer yes when the text says it, and give the reason in a sentence.
