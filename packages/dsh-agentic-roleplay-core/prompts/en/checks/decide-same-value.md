Two values a story keeps track of are described below, each with its name, key and what it means. Do they measure the same thing, so that keeping both would record one fact twice and let the two drift apart? They do when one is another name, abbreviation or translation of the other (health and hit points, gold and coins). They do not when they are related but different (health and stamina, trust and affection, hunger and thirst), or measure the same thing for different subjects. Answer yes when they are the same value, and give the reason in a sentence.
