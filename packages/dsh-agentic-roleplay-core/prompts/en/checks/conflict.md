Two background entries of a story world are given. Find whether they say things that cannot both be true at the same time (different dates for one event, different causes, a person alive in one and dead in the other, a place in two incompatible spots). Details that only add to each other, statements about different times, and the differing beliefs of different people are not conflicts. If they conflict, answer conflict true and quote, exactly as written, the passage of the first entry and the passage of the second that rule each other out, and say in a sentence why. If not, answer conflict false with empty quotes. Never conclude a conflict you cannot quote. A title or heading is not a statement, and two entries with similar titles are not in conflict for that reason; quote the clauses that disagree.
