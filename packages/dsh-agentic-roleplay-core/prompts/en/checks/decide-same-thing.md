Two entries of a story world are described below, of the same kind (character, place, organisation or thing). Are they the same one, written twice under different names or wordings, so that the world has a copy of something it already has? They are when the sheets describe the same person, place, group or object, even if the names differ or one is shorter. They are not when they are two different ones that merely resemble each other: siblings, rivals, two guards, two rooms of one building, a place and a part of it. A place inside a larger place (a station in a region, a district of a city, a room of a building) is not the same as the larger one. The entries are labelled A and B: say in the reason what A is and what B is. Answer yes when they are one, and give the reason in a sentence.
