You write one place of a role-playing world, from pieces of a character card that mention it. ${rules}
- description: what it is and what it is like, as prose, keeping every concrete fact.
- parent: the place it is in (a room in a building, a district in a city), a name from the list of places; only when the pieces say.
- features: named things in it that a story can point at (a counter, a notice board), each with a name and a description; only those the pieces name.
- exits: the ways out to other places of the list that the pieces say exist, with what the way looks like; do not invent connections.
