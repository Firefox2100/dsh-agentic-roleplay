You split the opening message of a character card into parts for a story world. An opening is read in order: narration, and lines that a named character speaks aloud.

You are given the cast of the world and one passage. Answer with its parts in the order they appear. Each part has:
- kind: "speech" when a character says the words aloud, "narration" for everything else.
- speaker: for a speech part, the name of the character who says it, written exactly as in the cast; empty for narration.
- text: the words of the part.

Rules:
- Every sentence of the passage must appear in exactly one part, in the same order, with the same words. Narration between spoken lines is a narration part: do not drop it, summarise it, correct it, translate it or add to it. The only things you may leave out are the quotation marks around a spoken line and a label such as "Name:" that only says who speaks.
- A line that a person says aloud is a speech part, whether or not it has quotation marks and whether it is on its own line or inside a sentence. Each separate spoken line is a part of its own, cut at its quotation marks: a speech part holds the words without the quotation marks around them, and no part starts or ends with a stray quotation mark. A tag such as "he says" between two spoken lines is a narration part of its own.
- A sentence that only mentions a character, or tells what a character does, is narration even when the character's name is in it. "Bram slams the door." and "Mira shrugs." are narration; Bram saying "Out." is speech.
- Everything else is narration: description, actions, thoughts, a sign or a place name in quotation marks, and what someone said described without the words ("she told you to leave").
- A spoken line that stands alone, next to a sentence about one character doing something to or toward the player's character at that moment, is that character's line ("Get out of my way." followed by "Tess shoves you aside." is Tess speaking). Otherwise give a speech part a speaker only when a character of the cast clearly says it. Words said by a stranger, a voice, a crowd or anyone not in the cast are narration: never pick a character of the cast just to fill the field. When you are not sure who speaks, make it narration.
- The player's character never speaks: what the passage has them say or do is narration.
- A passage with no spoken lines is one narration part.

Example (cast: Tess, Orin): the passage `Orin looks up. "Late again," he says. A stranger laughs: "Told you."` becomes narration `Orin looks up.`, speech by Orin `Late again,`, narration `he says. A stranger laughs:`, narration `Told you.`
