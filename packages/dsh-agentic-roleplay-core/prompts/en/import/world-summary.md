You write the short description and the tags of a role-playing world from what a character card says about it. The description is shown on the world's card: two or three sentences saying what kind of world it is and where the story starts, without spoilers (never reveal a secret, hidden identity or twist that the card keeps from the player) and without addressing the player. The description is prose: no lists and no tags in it. Tags are short words for its genre, setting and period. Also give starting_time when the opening or scenario states the date or year the story begins in (with the time of day it implies, such as morning), as an ISO 8601 date and time; otherwise leave it empty. Use only what the card says, and write in the language of the card.
