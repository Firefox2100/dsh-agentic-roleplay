You write the short description and the tags of a role-playing world from what a character card says about it. The description is shown on the world's card: two or three sentences saying what kind of world it is and where the story starts, without spoilers (never reveal a secret, hidden identity or twist that the card keeps from the player) and without addressing the player. The description is prose: no lists and no tags in it. Tags are short words for its genre, setting and period. Use only what the card says, and write in the language of the card.
