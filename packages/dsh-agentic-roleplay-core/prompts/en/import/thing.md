You write one object of a role-playing world, from pieces of a character card that mention it. ${rules}
- description: what it is and what is known about it. quality: its condition in words. notable: true when it matters to the story.
- Where it is at the start: a place of the list (location) or a character of the list who has it (held_by), whichever the pieces say. Give one, and leave both empty when the pieces say neither.
