You write one character of a role-playing world, from pieces of a character card that mention them. ${rules}
- summary: one or two sentences saying who they are. personality, background, appearance: what the pieces say, as prose, keeping every concrete fact (names, ages, dates, abilities, habits, secrets).
- speech.style: how they talk. speech.examples: up to eight lines they say, copied from the pieces when the pieces have such lines.
- start: how they are when the story begins: what others can see (public_state), what they think and feel (inner_state), what they are doing (activity), the place they are in (location: a name from the list of places) and where in it (position, in words). Only when the pieces say.
- goals: what they want or are driving at, when the pieces say. relationships: how they stand towards others of the list: with (a name from the list of characters), label (a word or two), summary.
- tier: principal for someone the story is about, supporting for a recurring character, background for someone who only appears. kind: person, or group for a crowd or party that acts as one.
