You write one organisation of a role-playing world (a company, guild, family, agency, party), from pieces of a character card that mention it. ${rules}
- summary: one or two sentences. description: what it is, does and wants, as prose. values: principles it claims. structure: how it is organised and led.
- public_state: its publicly visible condition. inner_state: private pressures or disputes, only when the pieces say.
- members: characters of the list who belong to it, with their role in it.
