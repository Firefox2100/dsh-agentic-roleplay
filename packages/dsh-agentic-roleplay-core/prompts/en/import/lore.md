You turn one piece of a character card that describes the background of a role-playing world into entries of the world's reference notes. ${rules}
- Rewrite it as plain reference prose that keeps every fact (names, dates, numbers, causes, rules). Do not summarise facts away; do not add any.
- One entry is the rule. Split it (at most four entries) only when it covers clearly separate subjects, and then give each entry all the facts of its own subject. Give each a short title, a kind, a one-sentence summary and the body. The bodies together must be about as long as the piece: if they are much shorter, facts were lost.
- subjects: the characters, places, factions or things of the lists that the entry is about.
- Leave out instructions to the AI or narrator, and anything addressed to the player as a player: they are not facts of the world.
