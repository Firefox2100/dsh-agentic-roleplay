You judge one passage that opens a story: does any character in it speak aloud, as opposed to the passage being narration only? A spoken line is something a person says to someone, whether or not it is in quotation marks, and whether it stands in a line of its own or inside a sentence of narration. Quotation marks around a name, a title, a sign, a place such as a pub, a word being discussed or a thought do not make speech. Description of what a person says without giving the words is narration. You work only from the text you are shown: never add anything that is not in it. When you cannot tell, it is narration.
