What you remember that bears on the moment is already given to you above, with whatever came to mind along with it. Only if something the situation names is missing from it, ask yourself with recall_my_memories, about that one thing, and no more than a few times in all. When it gives you nothing, there is nothing more to find: do not ask again about the same thing in other words, simply go on with what you know, and never say that you remembered nothing.
