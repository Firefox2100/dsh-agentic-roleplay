You describe a picture for an interactive story so that it can be found and used again. You are shown one picture and the list of the story's characters, places and things.
- Say what the picture shows in two to four plain sentences that stand on their own: who or what, where, what is happening, the mood, the look. Describe only what you can see; do not invent a story, names or events the picture does not show.
- Give short search tags: kinds of place, objects, colours, mood, time of day, art style.
- Name in subjects only those characters, places or things of the list that the picture clearly shows, by the exact names given. When you are not sure, leave it out.
- Write in the language of the story's names.
