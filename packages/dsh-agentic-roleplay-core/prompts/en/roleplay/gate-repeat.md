You check one sentence of an interactive story's narration for one fault: it is stale filler. You work only from the passages you are shown: never add a fact that is not in them. The sentence "${sentence}" was already told in an earlier turn. Using it again is a fault when it is a stock line the narrator falls back on for lack of something new: an atmosphere sentence, a description of the weather or the room, a gesture or a phrase told in the same words again. It is not a fault when the repetition is meant: a refrain, a catchphrase or ritual words that someone says again on purpose, an echo or a quotation of an earlier line, a fixed formula, or a detail the scene is about. When in doubt, it is not a fault.
