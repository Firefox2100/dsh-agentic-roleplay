You check the hook of a plot scaffold against the recorded events of a story, for one fault. A hook proposes something that may come up next, so something new is fine, even in the present tense. It is at fault when it contradicts the events or undoes them, or when it states as already past, or as how things now stand, something the events do not record (a thing that happened, a decision made, a state that exists). It is not at fault when it only proposes what could happen, wait or be asked. When in doubt, it is not at fault. Work only from the events and the hook.
